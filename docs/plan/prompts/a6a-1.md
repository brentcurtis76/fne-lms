The owner-reviewed brochure is canonical for tone and structure — mirror its
argument, in web form:

1. **Hero** — `COHORT_HEADLINE` as a date chip, plus `9 días de visitas ·
   7 escuelas`. Headline idea: living a school from the inside, not touring it.
   Primary CTA anchors to `#programa`; secondary CTA downloads the ficha
   (`/api/pasantias/ficha`).
2. **Por qué Barcelona** — the invitation, with `COHORT_CLAIMS` (400+ pasantes,
   40+ colegios, 12 escuelas catalanas en la red) as quiet proof, not a banner.
3. **Dos semanas, dos modos** — *the most persuasive idea in the program, so
   give it the most space*: semana 1 **inmersión** (2,5 días en Virolai + 2,5 en
   Sadako — you live one school, you don't visit it), then semana 2 **visitas**
   (1–2 escuelas/día; El Puig y Les Vinyes día completo, fuera de Barcelona).
4. **El fin de semana largo** — sáb 10 a lun 12, free, lunes 12 = Fiesta
   Nacional. **Frame it as a feature** (breathing room, or Europe), never as a gap.
5. **Cómo es un día** — `COHORT_DAY_STRUCTURE`'s three moments.
6. **Las siete escuelas** — two-tier, and say what each is known for if the
   module carries it; otherwise name + role + full-day marker.
7. **El equipo** — `COHORT_EXPERTS` with their exact titles (Sandra Entrena is
   **Encargada de Innovación**, Sergi del Moral is **Director** — both were
   corrected the hard way; do not paraphrase a title).
8. **Qué te llevas** — the 13 objectives. They are many; group or tier them
   visually, but **never edit their text**.
9. **FAQ** — ≥5, es-CL, **no prices**: quién puede ir, qué incluye el programa

## Craft constraints

- Compiled Tailwind + the repo's brand tokens — model `pages/registro-tractor.tsx`.
  **Do NOT copy the homepage's `cdn.tailwindcss.com` script**; that is a known
  defect, not a pattern.
- Reuse `components/Footer.tsx`.
- `<Head>`: es-CL title/description + OG/Twitter tags with an absolute URL via
  `lib/utils/app-url.ts` — this page will be shared on WhatsApp, so the unfurl
  matters as much as the page.
- Must read well on a phone first (school leaders will open it from a message)
  and on old school hardware — no heavy client JS.
- `data-testid` on every interactive element; es-CL copy throughout.
- Accessibility basics: real landmarks, one `h1`, headings in order, alt text,
  keyboard-reachable CTAs.
