## 2 — The brand system

`tailwind.config.js` already carries the palette and the names match the manual:
`brand_primary` #0a0a0a (Negro FNE) · `brand_accent` #fbbf24 (Amarillo FNE) ·
`brand_accent_hover` #f59e0b (Ámbar) · `brand_accent_light` #fcd34d ·
`brand_gray_dark` #1f1f1f (body text) · `brand_gray_medium` #6b7280 (secondary).
**Use the tokens, never raw hex.**

Two things are missing and you should add them: the **gold gradient**
(#FDB833 → #B47410 at 135°) and the interface greys 50–700 if you need them.

Rules that are not suggestions:

- **Proportion 60 white / 30 black / 10 yellow.** If the page reads "yellow", there
  is too much: cut it back to an accent and give the weight to black.
- **One solid-yellow element per surface.** Not two.
- **Black text on yellow, never white on yellow.**
- **The gradient is brand, not decoration.** Outside the logo it appears as a rule
  or an accent — never as a full background.
- `brand_accent` and `brand_accent_hover` are **surface** colours: on white they
  measure 1.66:1 and 2.14:1. They may not carry small text on a light background.
  For accent text on white use **`brand_accent_text`** (#b45309, 5.02:1). This is
  not a style preference — A6a r3 fixed 15 real contrast violations here.

### Typography

**Mont only** (already wired in `public/fonts/` with `@font-face` in globals.css;
Inter is the fallback if Mont fails to load, never a design choice).

| role | size/weight | notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display | 44 / 900 | always uppercase, tracking **−0.02em** (tight, not expanded) |
| H1 | 32 / 800 | |
| H2 | 24 / 700 | section titles, sentence case |
| H3 | 18 / 700 | |
| Body | 15 / 400 | line-height 1.6 |
| Small | 13 / 400 | captions, credits |
| Eyebrow | 11 / 600 | uppercase, tracking 0.08em |

Display headlines and short labels in CAPS; section titles in sentence case; no
English Title Case. **Accents are always preserved, including in caps** —
FUNDACIÓN, EDUCACIÓN, PASANTÍAS. Dropping them is an error, not a style.

### Icons and graphic elements

Lucide, **1.75px stroke, rounded caps, monochrome inheriting the text colour**,
at 16/20/24 — the stroke stays 1.75 at every size. No filled or two-colour icons.
Permitted graphic elements, and only these: a gold rule as a separator or section
underline; a short yellow bar under a page title; the lineal symbol as a watermark
at **≤12% opacity**.

## 3 — Photography — this is the point of the round

The manual is explicit that **photography is the only place the brand allows
abundant colour**, and the owner's specific ask is **more Barcelona**.

Already in the repo: `public/barcelona-skyline.jpg`, `barcelona-innovation.jpg`,
`barcelona-stats.jpg`, `barcelona-third.jpg`, `public/images/barcelona-skyline-photo.png`.
The owner is supplying more from the brochure — place new files under
`public/images/pasantias/` and keep the existing naming style.

**The canonical treatment** (manual p.14) is exactly this page's hero:

> full-bleed image · black gradient veil rising from the edge where the text sits ·
> yellow eyebrow · white Black-weight headline in caps. The veil reaches **85%
> opacity under the text and dissolves before the photo's centre of interest.**

Use it for the hero, and reuse the pattern for section breaks so the page has a
rhythm of colour instead of an unbroken wall of white cards.

- **Ratios:** 16:9 for web headers and covers · 1:1 · 4:5 for portrait.
- **Radius 12px** on contained cards and blocks; **no radius** when an image bleeds
  to the page edge.
- **Crop, never distort.** Centre the crop on the subject.
- **Text over an image always needs the veil**, white text, contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Text
  straight onto a photo is listed as an error in the manual.
- **One hero photograph per section.**
- **Never** duotone or yellow-tinted images, black and white, heavy grain, vignette,
  colour filters, collages, decorative frames or overlaid textures. The manual calls
  yellow tinting over a photo out specifically.
- The logo over a photograph is the **white** version, never the gradient, and needs
  a black veil of at least 40%.
- **Every image needs descriptive alt text in es-CL.** This is both brand rule and
  an accessibility gate.
- WebP or high-quality JPEG; PNG only where transparency is needed. Keep the files
  reasonably sized — this page is sold over WhatsApp on school hardware.

## 4 — The content is not yours to change

**Every fact still comes from `lib/pasantias/cohort-public.ts`.** No cohort string
is hardcoded in the page — not a date, school, level, highlight, expert name or
title. Add a test that greps the page for hardcoded cohort content and fails.

This is the load-bearing criterion. Seven rounds of guards exist because approved
facts drifted from that module; a redesign that inlines copy looks identical on day
one and rots silently the moment a fact changes. If a section needs data the module
does not expose, **add an export to the module** and pin it — do not type the text
into the page.

The copy itself (headline, section intros, FAQ answers) may be re-set typographically
but **not rewritten**. If you believe a line reads badly, raise it as a finding.

Voice, if you touch any connective copy: es-CL, second person, warm and direct;
action verbs; no emoji; Chilean number format (1.250); one idea per message.
