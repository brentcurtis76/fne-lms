## 1. THE DISCLOSURE RULING — read before designing

Dial-in is only useful if a human can complete the call, and that needs **three** things:
a phone number, the **meeting number**, and the **participant passcode**. The last two are
exactly what `zoom_internal` exists to contain.

**PM RULING 1 — all three may be returned by `pages/api/meet/session/[id]/join.ts`, and by
nothing else.**

That endpoint is the plan's *single authorized opening* through the trust boundary. It
already resolves the join policy and already returns `join_url` — which is
**passcode-embedded and therefore secret-equivalent** — to exactly the caller who is
entitled to join (`:26`, `:160`, `:180`). Returning the same secret in a form a human can
read aloud into a phone keypad is **the same disclosure to the same authorized caller
through the same opening**. It is not a new boundary crossing.

**What this ruling does NOT permit, and these are hard:**
- **No dial-in, meeting number or passcode in any notification payload**, ever. Chunk
  Z2-4a's invariant 2 stands untouched: notification payloads are persisted in an event log
  and rendered into e-mail.
- **No dial-in in any .ics file.** `lib/utils/session-ical.ts` documents at `:16-20` why it
  carries no raw meeting link; the same reasoning covers dial-in credentials exactly.
- **No column on `public.session_meetings_public`.** Z2-4d's `[F7]` asserts it has none;
  that assertion must keep passing.

If your design needs any of those three, **stop and report `FINDINGS`.**

**PM RULING 2 — render it on `/meet/session/[id]`, the dedicated join surface.**
`PLAN.md:187` says "dial-in numbers on session detail for school-outage audio fallback".
`pages/meet/session/[id].tsx` is the surface a participant actually lands on to join, it is
already behind the join policy, and it already fetches through `JoinMeetingButton` (`:31`,
`:83`). **The admin and consultor detail pages are OUT of scope for this chunk** — if you
believe the plan requires dial-in there too, note it under NOT DONE rather than building it.

cannot load `/meet/session/[id]` to read the number. So this chunk delivers what the plan
asks for and **does not, by itself, survive the scenario that motivates it** — the number
