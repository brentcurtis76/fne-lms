## 1. THE TWO RULINGS — settled, do not re-derive

**RULING A (owner, recorded in `PLAN.md` §5).** The plan previously said
`authorizeMeetingJoin()` governs the `/meet` page's `getServerSideProps`. r23 refused that
and was right: it would have re-tiered four roles as a side effect of a security fix. **The
intent is sharpened, not dropped:**

1. **Page visibility keeps `canViewSession`.** Do not change who can open the page. `docente`
   stays denied; `gcLeader` and `consultorGlobal` keep what they have. **If your diff changes
   any page-visibility tier, you have gone wrong.**
2. **`authorizeMeetingJoin()` governs the join CAPABILITY** wherever it appears on that page
   — the Zoom join affordance **and the raw pasted `meeting_link`**.
3. **The §14 kill switch is enforced in `/meet` SSR**, as §14 already required.

**RULING B (owner, this round).** Implementing (2) collides with a **mandatory** e2e spec:
`tests/e2e/zoom-join-authz.spec.ts:70-79` asserts that view-only personas **do** reach
`meet-join-link` and that its `href` equals the raw `LINKED.meetingLink`. That spec documents
the very inconsistency (2) removes — "view-only" currently means *no credentials* for a Zoom
session and *here is the link* for a pasted-link session.

**The owner has ruled: make it consistent.** View-only means the same thing for every
provider. **Those assertions invert.** A persona who may view but may not join gets **no join
affordance and no raw link**, whether the meeting is Zoom-managed or a pasted link.

## 2. WHY THE PASTED LINK IS THE SAME THING AS THE BUTTON

`pages/meet/session/[id].tsx:88-91` renders `<a href={session.meeting_link}>` for any session
carrying a link — `google_meet`, `teams`, `otro`. `resolveMeetSessionAccess` puts
`meeting_link` into page props (`lib/utils/session-meet-access.ts:119`).

Whoever holds that URL can enter the meeting. It is a **capability**, not information, and it
is currently gated only by page visibility while the Zoom path is gated by the join policy.
**Two ways into a meeting, two different rules, chosen by whichever provider the scheduler
happened to pick.** That is the defect.
