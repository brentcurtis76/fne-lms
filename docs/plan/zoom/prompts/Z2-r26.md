## 1. ITEM 6 — managed sessions tell nobody where to go

**PM-verified.** Every platform-link site gates on `meeting_link` being truthy:

- `lib/services/session-lifecycle-notifications.ts:281` —
  `join_url: session.meeting_link ? buildAbsoluteUrl(…) : null`
- `pages/api/cron/session-reminders.ts:109` — the same shape
- `pages/api/sessions/ical.ts:216`, `pages/api/sessions/[id]/ical.ts`,
  `pages/api/sessions/series/[groupId]/ical.ts`

**But a Zoom-managed session has `meeting_link = NULL` by design.** Z2-1 forces it null on
the managed transition (`pages/api/sessions/[id]/index.ts:394`) — the real join URL lives in
`zoom_internal` and never touches `consultor_sessions`.

**So for exactly the sessions this whole phase exists to serve, the notification says a
session was scheduled and offers no way to reach it; the reminder an hour before offers no
way to reach it; and every calendar file has no URL.** Chunks Z2-4a and Z2-4b were built to
tell people about a session, and for managed sessions they tell them nothing actionable.

**THE RULING.** Emit the platform link when **`meeting_link` exists OR `is_zoom_managed` is
true**. The link is always the platform surface — `buildAbsoluteUrl(buildSessionJoinPath(id),
req)` — **never the raw `meeting_link`, and never anything from `zoom_internal`.** That
invariant is untouched and `[N4]` re-proves it.

The platform URL is correct even before provisioning lands: `/meet/session/{id}` resolves the
current state per click, and r25 made that page refuse anyone who may not join.
