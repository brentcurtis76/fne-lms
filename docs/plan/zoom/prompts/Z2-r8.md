## 3. PM RULINGS — do not choose differently

1. **Dedupe keys.** `meeting_sync` keys on the *target* schedule —
   `meeting_sync:consultor_session:<id>:<starts_at_iso>:<duration>` — so a double-submit
   of one reschedule collapses to `'duplicate'` while a genuine later reschedule mints a
   fresh job. `meeting_delete` keys statically on the surface:
   `meeting_delete:consultor_session:<id>`. A surface is deleted once and never
   un-deleted, and the handler is idempotent, so a static key is correct here.
2. **A Zoom 404 on delete is SUCCESS.** The meeting is gone; that is the desired state.
   Complete the job, move the row terminal, publish the projection. Retrying a 404 to
   dead-letter would leave the projection permanently stale — the opposite of the goal.
3. **A Zoom 404 on sync is NON-RETRYABLE FAILURE, not success.** The meeting we believe
   we own does not exist. Record it on the row and fail the job terminally with a
   structured reason; do not silently re-create (re-provisioning is `meeting_provision`'s
   job and inventing a second creator here would defeat the §9 host reservation).
4. **No user action ever fails because of Zoom** (§8, same rule Z2-1 established for
   approve). Cancel, reschedule and modality-flip return exactly what they return today,
   with byte-identical status and body, whether the enqueue succeeded, deduped, was
   gated off, or threw. Enqueue **after** the session update commits, never before.
5. **The cancellation-clause flow is untouched.** `executeCancellation`,
   `evaluateCancellationClause`, `calculateNoticeHours`, the `devuelta`/`penalizada`
   ledger rows — read them if you must, change nothing. You are adding an enqueue beside
   that flow, not into it.
6. **Re-check eligibility on claim, not just at enqueue.** A job may run seconds or hours
   later. `meeting_sync` must refuse a session that has since been cancelled, and both
   handlers must refuse a surface with no `zoom_meetings` row. The queue is at-least-once;
   handlers own their own correctness.
7. **Use the projection RPC** (`sync_projection_from_meeting`), never a hand-written
   upsert. It is monotonic by design and refuses to move a projection backwards — that
   guard is load-bearing when an out-of-order webhook and a job race.
8. **No new migration.** If you believe one is needed, **stop and report FINDINGS** —
   the last chunk's migration is still unverified against production and a second
   unapplied one would compound exactly the failure that broke session approval in Z1b.
