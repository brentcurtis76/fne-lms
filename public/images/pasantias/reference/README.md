# Reference only — NOT shippable

Three Barcelona photographs supplied 2026-08-05 (Park Güell, Casa Milà, Eixample
aerial), cropped to 16:9 at their native resolution.

**These must not ship.** Two reasons:

1. **Resolution.** They are 620–1000 px wide. A full-bleed hero needs roughly
   2400–2880 px (a 1440 CSS-px viewport at 2×). The repo's `bcn-skyline.jpg` is
   2400 px, downsampled from a 7551 px original. Stretched to full width these
   would visibly soften — worst on the large displays a director is most likely
   browsing on.
2. **Provenance unknown.** They arrived at thumbnail dimensions typical of a
   search-result save. This page sells a paid programme, so every image on it
   needs a licence that permits commercial use, or to be FNE's own work.

They are kept because they are useful **compositional references** — they tell a
designer what the section should look like. Claude Design can lay out against
them. What ships has to be a properly licensed, high-resolution version of the
same idea.

## What to replace them with

- **FNE's own photography.** These trips run twice a year with 400+ alumni; the
  brand manual explicitly prefers real situations over stock, so team photos are
  both cheaper and more on-brand than anything bought.
- Failing that, Unsplash or Pexels — free, commercial use permitted, and both have
  strong Barcelona coverage at 4000 px and above. Search "Eixample", "Park Güell",
  "Casa Milà", "Barcelona golden hour".

Shippable assets live one directory up.
